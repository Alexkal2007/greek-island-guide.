Before you go

Useful travel information

Essential tips and practical insights to ensure a seamless holiday across the Aegean.

Best time to visit

May–June & September for pleasant weather and fewer crowds; July–August for peak summer life.

Currency & Payments

Euro (€) is used everywhere. Cards are accepted almost universally, but carry small cash for tiny island cafes.

Getting Around

Ferries link most islands, while scooters and cars are best for exploring hidden beaches on arrival.

Language

Greek is the official language, but English is widely spoken in hotels, restaurants, and shops.

Ferry Tickets

Book early for peak summer months (July–August), especially for high-speed catamarans and vehicles.

Health & Safety

Greece is very safe. Pharmacies are widely available, and European Health Cards (EHIC) are valid.

Electricity & Plugs

Standard voltage is 230V (50Hz) with Type C and F plugs. Non-EU travelers may need an adapter.

Sun & Meltemi Wind

Sunscreen and hydration are essential. Strong summer winds (Meltemi) in August can affect ferry schedules.

Tipping Culture

Tipping is not strictly mandatory but appreciated. Leaving 5%–10% in tavernas and cafes is common.

Mobile & Connectivity

EU roaming is free. Travelers from third countries can easily get a local prepaid SIM or eSIM.
