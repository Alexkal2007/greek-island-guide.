Frequently asked questions

Before you book your trip

What's the best time for island hopping in Greece?

May, June and September offer warm weather with fewer crowds and calmer ferry schedules. July and August are busier but come with the full summer atmosphere.

How many islands can I practically visit during a 10-day trip?

We usually recommend 3 to 4 islands for 10 days, so you have enough time in each destination without spending most of your trip travelling.

Do I need to rent a car on every island I visit?

It depends on the island's size. On larger islands like Crete or Rhodes it helps a lot, while on smaller islands buses or bicycles are often enough.

How far in advance should I book my ferry tickets?

You can compare routes and companies through our guide and book directly online, usually a few weeks ahead during the summer months.

What is the estimated average daily budget for a week in the Greek islands?

Budgets vary significantly by region. On popular destinations like Santorini or Mykonos, expect €150-€300+ per person daily including stay and dining. Mid-range islands like Naxos or Serifos average €70-€140 per day.

How should I handle potential ferry delays or cancellations due to high winds (Meltemi)?

Meltemi winds occur mostly in July and August. Larger conventional ferries handle rough seas much better than high-speed catamarans. Always keep a buffer day before your international flight back home.

Are the Greek islands family-friendly for travellers with toddlers or seniors?

Yes, many islands feature shallow sandy beaches and easy access. Islands like Naxos, Paros, Corfu, and Rhodes are exceptionally well-equipped for families, whereas steep cliffside villages like Oia in Santorini may involve many stairs.

How reliable is public transportation (KTEL buses) across different islands?

During the peak summer season (June-September), local KTEL bus networks connect main towns, ports, and popular beaches frequently. Schedules are usually synchronized with arriving ferries, making public transit very reliable.

What is the main difference between High-Speed Catamarans and Conventional Ferries?

High-speed ferries cut travel time in half but are more sensitive to sea weather and cost roughly 30-50% more. Conventional ferries offer open deck walking space, smoother rides, and lower fares.

Do I need to carry cash, or are credit cards widely accepted everywhere?

Cards are universally accepted in restaurants, hotels, and stores across Greece. However, carrying small cash (€10-€20) is recommended for small taxis, remote beach kiosks, or local village bakeries.
